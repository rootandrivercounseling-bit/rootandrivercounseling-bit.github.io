Relationship counseling

Couples Counseling in Boise, Idaho

You don't have to keep having the same fight.

Schedule a consultation

This may be a fit if...

  • You keep returning to the same unresolved argument
  • One of you pursues while the other withdraws
  • Trust, intimacy, or emotional connection has eroded
  • You want to understand the cycle instead of assigning blame

Relationships can become complicated quickly. Careers, children, financial pressure, parenting, changing priorities, individual growth, family dynamics, attachment patterns, intimacy, and the ordinary demands of life can gradually create distance between two people who once felt deeply connected.

Sometimes couples find themselves having the same argument over and over without ever actually resolving what is underneath it.

One person pursues. The other withdraws.

One feels unheard. The other feels criticized.

One wants more connection. The other feels overwhelmed.

Eventually, both people can begin to feel like their needs don't matter.

Conflict doesn't necessarily mean a relationship is broken. Repair is possible.

Slow down the cycle

Couples counseling can provide a space to slow down these patterns and understand what is actually happening between you. Rather than simply deciding who is right or wrong, we can work to understand the underlying needs, fears, attachment patterns, and emotional responses that keep the cycle going.

I help couples develop healthier ways to communicate what they're feeling and what they need without escalating the conflict or becoming defensive.

What we can work on together

  • Understand recurring conflict patterns
  • Improve communication and emotional connection
  • Rebuild trust and relational safety
  • Explore attachment patterns and emotional needs
  • Address intimacy and sexual connection
  • Navigate parenting, career, and life transitions
  • Learn how to repair after conflict
  • Reconnect with one another in meaningful ways

Create something more intentional

The goal isn't necessarily to recreate the relationship you had years ago.

Sometimes the relationship needs to become something new: more intentional, more honest, less defensive, and more connected.

My goal is to help couples in Boise understand one another more deeply, interrupt destructive patterns, and create the possibility for genuine repair and forward movement.
